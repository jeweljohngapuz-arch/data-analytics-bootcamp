Plain-English guide · the easiest way to use this system

What this is, in one paragraph

Two HTML files that work together as a 65-day BI Analyst transition system. The Dashboard tracks your daily work and the proof you produce. The Suite (companion file) drills you on terms, technical concepts, stakeholder scenarios, and interview prep. Goal: have enough real evidence — not just streaks — to land an entry-level BI / Data / Reporting Analyst role by Dec 31, 2026.

Your first 10 minutes today

1
Scroll to Weekly Overview. Find today's highlighted dot (amber = today). Click it.
2
Read the day card top-to-bottom once. Don't act yet. Just see the shape — what you'll build, what proof you'll save.
3
Look at Runway Control at the top. That's where you stand right now — days to deadline, applications sent, weakest skill.
4
Tap the + Add Job Posting button in Job Posting Alignment. Even before you start Day 1, log 1–2 real postings you'd want. It tells you what skills the market wants.
That's it. The rest is daily work. The system tracks proof, not effort. If you ship 65 honest days you'll be hireable.

Big idea

Streak ≠ proof. Hours studied ≠ hireability. What gets you hired: real artifacts (SQL files, dashboards, READMEs) + ability to explain them in plain English. This system rewards the artifacts.

Daily workflow — 4 steps

Every day looks the same. About 2–3 hours total. You don't need to optimize this — just follow it.

1
Open today's day card. Weekly Overview → click today's dot. The day detail panel expands inline.
2
Follow the steps in order. Days 1–7 use a 7-step pilot guide (See → Type → Break → Apply → Commit → Teach → Drill). Days 8+ use a 6-step rhythm (Anchor → Build → Deepen → Commit → Mae sentence → Drill). Each step has a time budget.
3
Fill the Day Complete Gate (6 boxes). Found near the bottom of the day card. Be honest — these gates feed your Hireability Score.
4
Mark Complete → Log Evidence. Hit the green "✓ Complete Day N" button. Commit confirms. An Evidence Log modal auto-pre-fills with the deliverable + your confidence/explanation scores. Save it.

Optional but recommended (20–30 min)

  • Run a Suite drill — purple button at the end of each day card opens the BI Analyst Suite. Today's context auto-syncs.
  • Check the Weak Concept Radar — if any concept has 3+ misses, drill it.

Weekly (Sundays)

  • Open Weekly Market Validation for the week that just ended. Fill the 8-item checklist + 2 reflection fields. Download as Markdown for GitHub.
  • Hit 📥 Export Progress in the warning banner at top. Save the JSON to OneDrive.

The proof loop — how it all connects

The whole system is one feedback loop. Doing the work creates evidence; evidence raises your Hireability Score; weak areas surface as the Next Best Action.

Day work → Gate → Evidence Log →
Hireability Score → Bootcamp Gates →
Runway Control & Next Best Action

Step by step

  1. You do the day's work — write SQL, build a Power BI page, draft a README.
  2. The Day Complete Gate (6 proof checkboxes) asks: did you actually do it? Honest answer required.
  3. If yes (all 6 checked) → marking complete auto-opens an Evidence Log entry pre-filled with the deliverable filename, skill, commit message.
  4. You add confidence (1–5) + explanation (1–5) scores. Save.
  5. The Hireability Score reads Evidence Log entries. Quality + count + breadth = your score.
  6. The score's 4 Bootcamp Gates (Fundamentals → Technical → Portfolio → Hiring) flip from amber to green as your proof builds.
  7. Runway Control pulls all of it together and tells you "Next Money Move" + flags warnings if proof is thin.
What breaks the loop: overriding the Gate without doing the work, inflating confidence/explanation scores, or marking complete without logging evidence. The system has warnings for all three. The system is only as honest as your inputs.

Why Portfolio Projects also feed Hireability

Each project marked Portfolio-ready adds +1.0 toward your Portfolio category target. Each Interview-ready adds +2.0. Saturating the Portfolio category lifts your overall score by up to +10%. Real projects matter more than logging many small artifacts.

How to do common tasks

Mark a day complete

  1. Scroll to the bottom of the day card.
  2. Check all 6 boxes in the Day Complete Gate (panel turns green at 6/6).
  3. Optionally pick Yes/No on "Did you use AI today?" — fills in AI reflection.
  4. Hit the green ✓ Complete Day N button.
  5. Commit modal opens. Copy the git command, run it in your terminal. Hit "Committed".
  6. Evidence modal auto-pre-fills. Add confidence + explanation scores + notes. Save.

Add a job posting

  1. Open 🎯 Job Posting Alignment.
  2. Hit + Add Job Posting. Fill company / role / URL (required) / priority.
  3. Card auto-expands. Hit + Add Requirement. Paste a skill they want. Mark proof status. Save.
  4. Repeat for 3–7 requirements. Match % calculates live.
  5. Click the proof pill on any requirement to cycle No → Partial → Yes → No.

Log evidence manually (outside the day flow)

  1. Open 📒 Evidence Log.
  2. Hit + Add Evidence. Fill date · skill · proof type · filename · confidence · explanation. Save.

Run a Gemini interview rep

  1. Open 🎤 Mock Interview · Gemini Handoff.
  2. Pick one of 5 prompts. Hit 📋 Copy Prompt.
  3. Open Gemini (or ChatGPT Voice / Claude Voice). Paste. Enable voice. Do the rep out loud.
  4. Back in dashboard, hit ✓ Log Rep. Evidence modal pre-fills. Set scores. Add weakest answer + next improvement in notes. Save.

Drill a weak concept

  1. Open 🪫 Weak Concept Radar. See top 3 weakest.
  2. Click 🎯 Drill this concept on any. Suite opens in a new tab with a red drill banner.
  3. Start any stage in Suite — queue is filtered to questions matching that concept only.
  4. Click ✕ Exit Drill Mode when done.

Mark a project Portfolio-ready

  1. Open 📦 Portfolio Projects. Pick a project.
  2. Hit ▸ Show rubric. Check items as you complete them honestly.
  3. At 6–9 checked: status flips to "Technically complete" — warning shows what's still missing.
  4. At 10–11 checked: status flips green "Portfolio-ready".
  5. At 11/11 + 2 walkthrough reps logged: hit Mark Interview-ready (purple).

Fill weekly market validation (Sundays)

  1. Open 📅 Weekly Market Validation. Navigator defaults to the week that ended.
  2. Fill Q1 ("What did the market ask for?"). Check all 8 boxes honestly. Fill Q2 ("What proof do I have, what's still weak?").
  3. Hit Mark Week Complete. Then 📥 Download as Markdown. Commit the .md file to GitHub.

Save your work — the rules

Everything lives in your browser's localStorage. No cloud sync. If you clear browser data, all progress is gone. Export weekly. Minimum.

How to export

  1. Tap the orange Export Now button in the warning banner at the top of the page (or the 📥 Export Progress button at the bottom).
  2. A JSON file downloads: jewel_progress_YYYY-MM-DD.json.
  3. Save it to OneDrive (or any cloud folder you trust).

How to import

  1. At bottom of page → 📤 Import Progress → pick a JSON file.
  2. Confirm. State merges into the current dashboard.

How to switch laptops

  1. Old laptop: Export → file goes to OneDrive.
  2. New laptop: open the dashboard URL → Import → pick the JSON from OneDrive.
  3. Everything restored: day status, evidence, jobs, projects, gate state, validations, runway settings.

What gets exported

  • All 65 day statuses + gate proof boxes + AI reflections
  • Pomodoro sessions + settings
  • Per-day learning notes
  • Evidence Log entries
  • Portfolio Project rubric state
  • Job Postings + requirements
  • Runway settings (Next Money Move)
  • Weekly Validations

What does NOT get exported

  • Suite data (separate localStorage; Suite has its own reset)
  • Weak Concept Radar data (shared key, lives across both files — separate from main export)

Rules & honest-use principles

The system is only as honest as your inputs. Inflating scores or overriding gates without doing the work breaks the loop and produces a Hireability Score you can't defend in interviews.

Be honest with the Day Complete Gate

  • Only check a box if it's actually true.
  • Overrides are allowed but flagged. Runway Control warns at ≥3 override-completed days.
  • Better to leave a day at 4/6 honestly than to pass 6/6 dishonestly.

Confidence and Explanation scores

  • Confidence 1–5: how solid you feel with the skill behind this artifact.
  • Explanation 1–5: can you explain it to a non-technical person right now, no notes? Mae test.
  • If you can't, score it 1–2 and revisit later.

Reset buttons

  • All destructive buttons use confirm-twice. Click once → button turns red and says "Confirm Reset". Click again within ~4 sec to commit, otherwise it disarms.
  • Reset to Defaults (bottom of page): wipes day status only. Doesn't touch Evidence Log, Projects, Jobs, etc.
  • Reset Evidence (inside Evidence Log): wipes only evidence entries.
  • Reset weak-concept data (inside Weak Concept Radar): wipes only Suite-miss tracking.

Drill Mode in Suite

  • Activating drill mode filters Suite queues to one concept only.
  • It stays active until you click ✕ Exit Drill Mode in the Suite's red banner.
  • Set new drill: clicking another concept on dashboard auto-replaces.

The "Technically complete ≠ Portfolio-ready" rule

A project at 6–9/11 rubric items is technically complete but not portfolio-ready. README, walkthrough, insights, and recommendation are usually the last 4 items — and they're what recruiters actually read. Don't skip them.

The BI Analyst Suite (companion file)

Separate HTML. Opens via the purple button at the end of each day card, or directly. It's optional reinforcement, capped at 20–30 min/day. Main work happens in the Dashboard.

How drilling works

  1. Open Suite. Pick a stage. Answer questions (3 hearts; lose all = end run).
  2. Every miss is classified by concept (JOIN logic, DAX filter context, etc.) and written to shared storage.
  3. Back in Dashboard, 🪫 Weak Concept Radar shows your top 3 weakest with full explanation cards.
  4. Click "Drill this concept" → Suite reopens filtered to that concept only. You drill until the misses drop.

Today's context sync

When you open the Suite, a blue banner shows today's bootcamp context pulled from the Dashboard. The recommended drill matches what you learned today.

Suite is reinforcement, not replacement. If you only do Suite drills and skip the Dashboard's daily build tasks, your Hireability Score stays low — quizzes don't create artifacts.
